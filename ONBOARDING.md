### Skills to Know About
- [/refine-idea] — Stress-tests a plan or design by interviewing you until each branch of the decision tree is resolved. Used here for charter/spec shaping before committing to implementation.
- [/opsx:explore] — Enters OpenSpec explore mode: a thinking partner for investigating problems and clarifying requirements before proposing a change.
- [/opsx:apply] — Implements tasks from an approved OpenSpec change.
- [/opsx:propose] — Creates a new OpenSpec change with design, specs, and tasks in one step.
- [bd (beads)] — Issue tracker used for ALL task tracking on this project. Run `bd ready` to find work, `bd show <id>` to view, `bd update <id> --claim` to claim. Do NOT use TodoWrite or markdown TODO lists.
